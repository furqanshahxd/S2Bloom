Nice To Meet You

Two sisters, one flower cart, fifteen years later.

"We never wanted to be the biggest shop in town — just the one that smells like an actual garden."

S2 Bloom & Flowerista started as a Saturday market stand in 2009 and grew into a full studio without ever giving up the habit of buying direct from growers within a hundred miles. Every stem that comes through our door is inspected, conditioned and arranged by hand the same day — nothing sits in a cooler waiting for an order.
